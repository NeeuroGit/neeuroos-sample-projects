◆ GetEEGImpedance()

float[] NSB_Manager.GetEEGImpedance ( )

EEG impedance helps to verify if the signal quality receiving from the 4 contact points/electrodes are good. Example, if there is makeup or some other impurities on the skin, this impedance values will be much higher, due to the contribution of more resistance between the electrodes and skin surface, and the EEG signals will not be good/accurate. Good impedance range is below 800kohms

Returns
Received EEG impedance
